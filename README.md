Recently earned my Ph.D from University of Michigan in Mechanical Engineering and Scientific Computing :-) - Go Blue! Now postdocing at USC in sunny Los Angeles.

I work on topics in uncertainty quantification with a particular interest in building surrogate models in the small / multi-event data setting. This is a common motif of scientific and engineering applications where running high-fidelity simulators is prohibitively expensive, and emulators need to take advantage of model hierarchies as well as domain knowledge for better cost accuracy trade-offs. Currently exploring:

- Active learning
- Neural ODEs and related methods for dynamical systems
- Techniques in optimal transport and flow matching
- Multi-fidelity approximate Bayesian Inference
- Rough paths
- (collaboration) Adaptive Covariance Estimation Techniques
- Satisficing solutions

I am also interested in explainable methods and how they can be potentially incorporated into NN surrogates for PDEs. Please reach out if any of these topics interest you and you'd like to brainstorm ideas / collaborations --- this is a particularly rewarding aspect of research for me.
